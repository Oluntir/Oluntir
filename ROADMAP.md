# Roadmap

**Version:** 1.1.0  
**Language:** English  
**Status:** Stable  
**Applies to:** Oluntir 1.1.0  
**Last updated:** 2026-07-29

## Current status: 1.1.0

Oluntir 1.1.0 provides project creation, project handling, recurring content elements and regions, and the three documented export modes.

## Before 1.0 stable

- regression testing of project creation, restore, and export;
- browser compatibility checks;
- verification of archive and folder exports;
- validation of English and German documentation links;
- review of license and third-party notices;
- correction of release-blocking defects only.

## After 1.0 stable

Potential later work may include editor completion features, improved diagnostics, project navigation, and additional framework profiles. These items are not commitments and are not part of version 1.0.0.
